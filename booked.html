Step 3: Get Your ABN Before We Speak

This is the one step people skip — and it's the one that wastes the call.

Your call is a working session. Your specialist builds your store with you, live, while you're on the phone.

To finish that setup in Australia you need an ABN — an Australian Business Number. Think of it like a numberplate for your business: a car needs a plate before it can legally drive, and a business needs an ABN before it can properly take money.

It's an 11-digit number from the Australian Government. It's free. It doesn't make you a company, it costs nothing to hold, and you keep it whether you build the store or not.

Show up without one and we can't finish. Show up with one and you walk away done.

Have These Ready

  • Your Tax File Number (TFN). Not strictly required, but including it is what gets your ABN issued on the spot instead of sent to review.
  • Your full legal name and date of birth — exactly as the ATO has them.
  • Your address. Home address is fine.
  • Your email and phone number.
  • A start date. Use today's date.
  • What your business will do. See point 4 below for the exact wording.

How To Apply — About 10 Minutes

  1. Go to the Australian Business Register using the button below and start a new ABN application.
  2. Choose "Individual / Sole trader" as your business structure. That's what you are — you don't need a company to start.
  3. Enter your details — name, date of birth, TFN, address. Match them to your ATO records or the system won't be able to identify you.
  4. For your business activity, put online retail / e-commerce. That's what you're doing: selling physical products through an online store.
  5. Set your start date to today. Don't post-date it — you need it active for the call.
  6. Submit. If the system can identify you, your 11-digit ABN appears on screen straight away. Screenshot it.

Can I Do This With A Full-Time Job Or While I'm Studying?

What Do I Need Ready For The Call?

A laptop or desktop, a quiet 45 minutes, your email inbox open, and your ABN sorted.

That's it. Turn up with those four things and the call runs start to finish.

Question 5

Does The ABN Cost Anything?

No. Applying through the Australian Business Register is free. There are sites that charge $50 to $100 to fill in the same form for you — you don't need them. Use the button in Step 3.

Question 6

Will An ABN Make Me Pay More Tax?

An ABN is a registration, not a tax. It identifies your business. You pay tax on what you actually earn, the same as always — and if you earn nothing, there's nothing to pay tax on.

We're not accountants and this isn't tax advice. If your situation is complicated, ask one.

Question 7

Do I Need A Business Name Or GST Registration Too?

Not to get started. As a sole trader you can trade under your own name with just the ABN. GST registration is generally only required once your turnover reaches the threshold — you're nowhere near that on day one.

Question 8

I'm Under 18 Or I Don't Have A TFN

You can still start the application without a TFN — it just goes to manual review instead of issuing instantly. Start it anyway, then bring it up on your call so your specialist knows where you're at.

Question 9

I'm Not In Australia — What Do I Do?

Every country has its own version of this: a business or tax registration number that lets your store take payments. The name changes, the idea doesn't.

Don't apply for anything yet. Requirements vary a lot country to country and getting the wrong one wastes your time. Turn up to your call and your specialist will tell you exactly which one you need and walk you through it.
