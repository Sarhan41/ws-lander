Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Winning Streak ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you apply for or use our free e-commerce course and related services through winningstreakecom.com (the "Site").

Who we are

Winning Streak provides a free e-commerce training course and helps applicants set up the tools to get started. You can contact us any time at support@winningstreakecom.com.

Information we collect

When you complete the application form on our Site, we collect the information you provide, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your country

We also collect basic technical information automatically when you visit the Site, including your device and browser type, your IP address, the pages you view, and identifiers stored in cookies.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Contact you about your application and help you get set up with the free course;
  • Reach you by phone call and email to assist with onboarding, and by SMS text message only if you opt in;
  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services;
  • Comply with our legal obligations.

SMS / text message consent

We only send SMS text messages to people who explicitly opt in by ticking the SMS consent checkbox on our application form. SMS consent is optional, unchecked by default, and is not required to submit the form, make a purchase, or use the free course. If you opt in, Winning Streak may text you about your application, onboarding, and setup. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for help. Opting out of SMS will not affect your ability to use the free course. Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of any purchase.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We share information only with service providers who help us operate — for example our CRM (Close), messaging and calling providers, email delivery, and the advertising and analytics providers described below — and only so they can perform services on our behalf. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cookies, advertising and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies on the Site to understand how it is used and to measure the performance of our advertising.

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) advertising. We use the Meta Pixel and Meta's Conversions API. These record actions you take on the Site — such as viewing a page or submitting the application form — and send them to Meta Platforms, Inc. so we can measure how our ads perform and show ads to people likely to be interested in the free course.

To help Meta recognise you as the same person who saw an ad, we may send a limited set of details alongside those actions, including your email address, phone number, first and last name, country, IP address and browser information. Your email address, phone number and name are converted into an irreversible code (a cryptographic hash) before they are sent — Meta does not receive them in readable form. Some of this information is sent from your browser and some directly from our servers, which is why turning off cookies alone may not stop it.

Site analytics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and understand which pages perform well, and Vidalytics to host and measure the videos on the Site.

Your choices. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings. You can also manage how your data is used for advertising in your Meta account settings, and opt out of interest-based advertising through industry tools such as optout.aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.com. Blocking cookies will not stop you using the Site or applying for the course.

If you are in the EU or UK, our lawful basis for this processing is your consent where required, and otherwise our legitimate interest in measuring and improving our advertising. You can withdraw consent or object at any time by emailing support@winningstreakecom.com.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. You may ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may withdraw consent to be contacted, by emailing support@winningstreakecom.com.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version.
