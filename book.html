Quick Questions

Question 1

Do I Need Any Experience?

No. Most people who book this call have never sold anything online. That's exactly why the setup happens on a call instead of in a course.

If you can open a browser and follow along, you can do this.

Question 2

Can I Do This With A Full-Time Job Or While I'm Studying?

Yes. The setup happens on one call. What you do after that runs on your schedule — some people work on it an hour a night, some only on weekends.

You just need to be at a computer for the call itself.

Question 3

Is This A Sales Call?

No. Your specialist is there to get your store built and working. That's the job.

Question 4

What If I Need To Reschedule?

Reply to your confirmation email and tell us — we'll move you. What we can't do is hold a slot for someone who doesn't show and doesn't say anything. Those get released the same day.
